Privacy Policy - Jelly Fusion

Effective date: October 9, 2026

GameShape ("we", "us") built Jelly Fusion (the "App") as an ad-supported free game. This page explains what information is collected when you use the App.

Information we collect

We do not ask you for, and do not store, any personal information such as your name, email or phone number. The App has no account system. Your game progress is stored only on your device.

Third-party services

The App uses Google AdMob to show advertisements. AdMob may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interaction data and app usage information
  • Diagnostic, crash and performance data

This information is used to deliver, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. See Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time by clearing the App's data or reinstalling the App.

Opting out of personalised ads

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android device settings under Settings > Privacy > Ads.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data security and retention

We do not keep any personal data on our servers. Data collected by AdMob is handled under Google's retention policies.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
